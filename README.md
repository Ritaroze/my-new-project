# AI Mood Guardian

## Summary
AI Mood Guardian is a friendly tool I built for the Building AI course. It helps users understand and manage their emotions by detecting moods from text, voice, or facial expressions, and offers practical relaxation or motivation tips based on the results. The goal is to make mental health support more accessible for anyone—students, professionals, or anyone who wants to better understand their own feelings.

## Motivation
Everyone faces stress and mood swings, and I often realized my own emotions too late. I wanted to create a supportive AI “guardian” that helps me—and others—quickly check in with our feelings and get useful suggestions, whether that’s a calming exercise, short meditation, or a quick pep talk.

## Project Highlights
- Detects emotion from text, voice, and facial expression (webcam optional)
- Personalized suggestions for relaxation or motivation
- Lets users rate suggestions to improve future recommendations
- Prioritizes privacy: supports local processing of audio/video, with anonymized data
- Tracks emotional trends so you can spot patterns in your feelings over time

## What I Learned
Working with emotion data is a huge challenge! Feelings are subtle and complex, and public datasets often lack cultural diversity. I hope in the future to collect more local data and work with mental health experts to make the suggestions even more considerate and relevant.

## How It Works
1. Select your input mode—text, audio, or webcam.
2. The AI detects your mood and instantly provides a suggestion.
3. Rate the suggestion to improve the system for yourself and future users.
4. You can review your emotional trends as a chart over time.

## Technology Stack
- NLP for text sentiment analysis  
- CNNs for facial emotion recognition  
- Deep learning for speech emotion analysis  
- A custom recommendation engine fine-tuned with user feedback

## Roadmap
- Integrate with chat apps (LINE, Messenger, etc.)
- Collect emotion data from local users for more culturally relevant insights
- Enhance analytics and long-term emotional tracking
- Work with psychology professionals for safer, smarter suggestions
